    About the Operator

    Denki Control Lab is operated based on practical experience in electrical work, equipment maintenance, and industrial control.

    The site aims to organize practical notes about electrical control, PLC basics, circuits, sensors, and tools in a way that is easier to understand for beginners and field workers.

    The operator has experience in automotive maintenance, equipment maintenance, and electrical work, and creates articles with a focus on practical field viewpoints and points that are easy to misunderstand during troubleshooting.

    Qualifications / training:

    • Second-Class Electrician in Japan
    • Certified Electrical Work Specialist in Japan
    • Second-Class Automobile Mechanic in Japan
    • Hazardous Materials Engineer Class B in Japan
    • Low-voltage electrical work safety training
    • Forklift operation training
    • Aerial work platform operation training
    • Slinging work training
    • Crane-related training
    • Organic solvent work chief training
    • Various industrial safety trainings

    The site focuses not only on technical terms, but also on how to think through practical electrical control problems safely and logically.
